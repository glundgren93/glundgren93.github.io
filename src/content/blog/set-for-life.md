---
title: "Set for life?"
description: "Recent thoughts"
date: 2026-11-19
---

I started working as a software engineer when I was 18 years old. Starting a career this early helped me in many ways. One of the most important was that it helped me become more self-confident. This didn't happen all of a sudden. It took me many years to realize that I was good at what I was paid to do. Sometimes I still wonder if this is actually true, though. But I'm mainly writing this to say that all these years working with software helped shape my identity in many ways. It made me realize that I have a bunch of different qualities, and this has really helped me overcome my insecurities.

Through my career, I moved abroad, traveled to many countries, and realized many childhood dreams. For all these years, I thought that I was, in a way, set for life. I thought that, thanks to God, I had chosen the right career path. However, this has changed recently. With the rise of AI, I'm not sure that is true anymore. I don't know what will happen in a couple of years. And this scares me a lot. I have often found myself asking all sorts of questions, such as:

- What will happen if I lose this part of my life?
- What would I even do?
- Would I need to go back to Brazil?

I've decided to share a bit about how I've been feeling. I don't know the answers or what will happen. But for now, the only thing I can do is continue to adapt to this new reality without worrying too much. One thing this experience has taught me is that I'm good at adapting. Let's hope this stays the same.
